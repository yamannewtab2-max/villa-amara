01 — The Estate

A house built for
the last hour of light

Four hectares · Ayung valley
1,200 m above the sea

Villa Amara was raised from local alang-alang, river stone and old teak — a compound of pavilions that open completely to the valley, so the garden and the rooms are one continuous space.

Every pavilion holds its own plunge pool and a shaded daybed. Breakfast appears when you wake. The rest of the day belongs to you.
